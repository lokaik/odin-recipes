Meatloaf

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, breadcrumbs, onion, egg, milk, ketchup Worcestershire sauce, salt , pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Mix everything together with your hands or a wooden spoon until just combined. Don't overmix.
  4. Shape the mixture into a loaf and place it in a lightly greased loaf pan or on a lined baking sheet.
  5. Mix the topping ingredients together and spread the mixture over the top of the meatloaf.
  6. Bake for 55-65 minutes, or until the center reaches 160°F (71°C).
  7. Let the meatloaf rest for about 10 minutes before slicing.
  8. Serve with mashed potatoes, vegetables, or whatever sounds good.

Brownies

Description

These classic homemade brownies are rich, fudgy, and easy to make. Made with cocoa powder, butter, sugar, and chocolate chips, they have a soft, chewy center and a slightly crisp top. They're perfect for a simple dessert or afternoon treat.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Grease an 8inch square baking pan or line it with parchment paper
  3. in a large bowl, whisk together the melted butter and sugar.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla extract and mix until smooth.
  5. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, cocoa powder, and salt.
  6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and stir until just combined.
  7. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  8. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan and spread it evenly.
  9. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comesout with few moist crumbs.
  10. Let the brownies cool before cutting them into squares and serving.
